Title : Computational Thinking
Article: https://dl.acm.org/doi/fullHtml/10.1145/1118178.1118215#top
Summary:
Computational thinking is a skill that everyone should have.
Problems can be solved by breaking them down and focusing on the relevant parts of the problem.
Within computer science, problems are solved by considering the worst-case scenario. This can be done with methods such as redundancy or error correction.

In this example of a banking system, programmers find any bugs in the system. Different ways to fix these issues can be investigated, and ways to let the system recover after these issues can be provided.
